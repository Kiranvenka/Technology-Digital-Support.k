# Technology and Digital Support - Community Service Project Report

## Project Title
Technology and Digital Support - Helping People Access Online Services

## Location
Anantapur, Andhra Pradesh, India

## College
GATES Institute of Technology

## Duration
8 Weeks

## Theme
Digital Literacy, Digital Payments, Government Online Services, Cyber Security Awareness, Financial Inclusion, and Digital Empowerment.

## Mission
To empower citizens through digital education, improve access to online services, promote safe digital practices, and encourage digital inclusion.

## Weekly Summary
1. Village Survey and Community Interaction
2. Smartphone Basics and Digital Literacy
3. UPI and Mobile Banking Awareness
4. Digital Payment Demonstrations
5. Cyber Security Awareness
6. QR Code Payment and Merchant Training
7. Government Online Services Awareness
8. Review, Feedback, and Impact Assessment

## Key Outcomes
- Improved access to online services
- Better awareness of government schemes
- Increased confidence in digital payments
- Enhanced cyber security awareness
- Greater digital inclusion
- Reduced fear of online transactions
